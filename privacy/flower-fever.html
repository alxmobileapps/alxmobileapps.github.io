Flower Fever — Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026 · Version 1.0

Short version: your progress is saved on your phone. Google sign-in and cloud save are optional. We use Google Firebase (sign-in, cloud save, analytics), Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play (purchases). We do not sell your information, and you can delete your account in the Game.

1. Who we are

Flower Fever (the Game) is provided by Alexander Martin Castillo, operating as ALX Mobile Apps, Philippines (we, us or our). This policy explains what information the Game handles, why, and the choices you have. Contact: alx.mobileapps@gmail.com.

App identifier: com.alxmobileapps.flowerfever.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress is saved on your phone: levels and stars, diamonds, lives, garden (plants, coins, water, house and garden visitors), settings (sound, music, vibration, voice), and whether you bought No Ads or the starter deal. It stays on your device unless you sign in (section 3). Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data removes it.

Reminder notifications (for example, when your lives are full or a daily gift is ready) are scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off in your phone's settings.

3. Optional Google sign-in and cloud save

Signing in with Google is optional. If you sign in, we use Google Firebase Authentication to identify your account and receive your Google account name, email address and a unique account ID. Your game progress (the same information described in section 2) is then saved to Google Cloud Firestore under your account ID, so you can continue on another phone. This data is stored by Google in the Singapore region (asia-southeast1).

Only you can read or change your cloud save; our security rules block access by other players. You can sign out at any time in Settings, and you can delete your account and cloud save with Settings → Delete my account and cloud save (see section 8).

4. Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the Game is played and to improve it, for example which levels are hard, when players stop, and whether features work. It collects gameplay events (such as level started, level won, purchase completed, garden actions) and standard device and app information, including an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system, app version, language, and approximate location derived from your IP address. We do not use analytics to identify you by name.

5. Advertising

The Game shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner during levels and in the garden, full-screen ads between some levels, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a reward. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Where required (for example in the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland), the Game shows Google's consent form so you can choose whether ads may be personalised. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings. Players who buy No Ads do not see banner or between-level ads; optional rewarded ads stay available.

Google's use of this information is described in its policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

6. Purchases

In-app purchases (diamond packs, the starter deal and No Ads) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your card or payment details. We receive confirmation of what was bought so the Game can deliver it, and Google Play provides purchase history for restoring No Ads.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers described above (Google Firebase, Google AdMob and Google Play), who process it to provide their services, and where the law requires it.

8. Keeping and deleting your information

Information on your device stays until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Your cloud save and sign-in account are kept while your account exists. To delete them, use Settings → Delete my account and cloud save in the Game, or email alx.mobileapps@gmail.com with the subject "Flower Fever account deletion" from the Google account you used; we will delete the account and cloud save within 30 days. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months under Google Analytics settings. Ad-related data is handled under Google's retention policies.

9. Children

Flower Fever is a casual puzzle game intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has signed in or provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and the EU/UK GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Email us to make a request; we may ask for information needed to verify it. You may also complain to your data-protection authority, such as the Philippine National Privacy Commission.

11. Security

Information sent to Google's services is protected in transit with encryption, and cloud saves are protected by access rules tied to your account. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

12. Changes

We may update this policy when the Game or the law changes. The effective date below shows the latest version, and we will give notice of important changes in the Game or its store listing.

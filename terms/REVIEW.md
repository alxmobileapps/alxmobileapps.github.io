# Terms of Service review pack

Prepared 5 November 2026 for the five apps currently present in ALX Mobile Apps' Play Console. These are review drafts, not adopted terms. Merging as written still leaves every page clearly labeled as a draft. Do not submit draft links in Play Console.

## Scope

- AlphaBlast: optional diamond and ad-removal purchases, profiles, challenges, backup-code/cloud restoration and platform-dependent sign-in.
- Circus Dogs, Circus Cats, Circus Fish and Circus Clowns: earned coins and character unlocks, device-local progress, ads and optional score sharing. No new real-money purchase functionality is represented.

## Before adoption

Approve the text and effective date, confirm the provider and contact details, and verify the actual purchase promises in each released build. Replace the draft notices and page titles with final terms and an effective date. Publish through the existing GitHub Pages site, verify each rendered URL, then enter the corresponding app-specific URL in Play Console. Add an accessible in-app link and obtain any legally required agreement/consent before relying on terms for users. These documents alone do not implement consent, change trader status or complete the blocked phone verification.
